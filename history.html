History & Honors

From a field at Anfield in 1892 to twenty league titles and six European Cups.

Our story

1892

Liverpool FC is founded by John Houlding after Everton leave Anfield. The club moves in. Begins its life as a separate team.

1901

The first league championship arrives, the start of a long list of titles.

1959

Bill Shankly becomes manager and rebuilds the club. He lays the foundation for its successful decades.

1977

Liverpool win their first European Cup in Rome then retain it the following year.

2005

The comeback in Istanbul: Liverpool recover from 3-0 down against AC Milan and win the Champions League on penalties.

2019 and 2020

A sixth European Cup in Madrid followed by the clubs first Premier League title and a 19th league championship.

2025

Liverpool win the Premier League again their 20th league title.
